Shared approach

Models as disciplined decision environments

The projects use computation to expose assumptions, compare alternatives, and test system responses—not to conceal uncertainty behind a single score.

01 / REPRESENT

Explicit mechanisms

Agents, networks, hazards, institutions, and constraints are represented as inspectable model components.

02 / EVALUATE

Multiple criteria

Performance, risk, cost, distribution, and robustness are kept distinct where collapsing them would hide trade-offs.

03 / INTERPRET

Bounded claims

Build checks, simulations, sensitivity tests, and empirical validation are reported as different forms of evidence.

Research integrity by design

Every public result will be paired with its scenario assumptions, evaluation metric, and validation scope. Stylized experiments are presented as mechanism tests and structured counterfactuals—not as literal forecasts of a specific city or economy.
