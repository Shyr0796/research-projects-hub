One logic across three domains

The intervention target changes, but the research discipline remains consistent: construct the system, define competing outcomes, test explicit interventions, and state what the evidence does and does not establish.

Shared approach

Models as disciplined decision environments

Explicit mechanismsAgents, fields, flows, constraints, and institutional rules are stated before outcomes are interpreted.
Multiple criteriaPerformance is not compressed into a single headline metric when important objectives conflict.
Comparable experimentsShared states, paired scenarios, and fixed evaluation definitions support interpretable contrasts.
Bounded conclusionsValidation, robustness, uncertainty, and generalization are reported as separate evidential questions.

Research integrity by design

These websites are interpretive companions to the manuscripts, not substitutes for them. Every principal figure links to its original paper-format PDF where available, numerical claims are tied to the stated experiment, and limitations remain visible beside the conclusions.
